## Location Display region names

`zones.tsv` is a selected, reformatted subset of `Locations.json` from Location Display.
Source: https://github.com/trinhc2/Location-Display/blob/035eb9a83a158ab1a40a2fc32a1f6d0435bad9a7/src/main/resources/Locations.json
The source credits mejrs' RuneScape Region Grid. The data uses approximate 64-tile
cells, not exact city boundaries. Duplicate coordinates were collapsed; conflicting
cells are left unnamed. No Location Display artwork, sounds or code is bundled.
